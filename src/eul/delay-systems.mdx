# Delay Systems

*Sébastien Boisgérault — Mines Paris, PSL University*

[← Back to Eul.Ink](index.html)

## About

Systems of delay-differential algebraic equations (DDAE) – or delay systems
for brevity – combine differential and algebraic equations with delayed
variables in the right-hand side.

as the [Smith predictor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_predictor).
